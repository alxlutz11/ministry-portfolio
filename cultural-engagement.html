SMART Goals

Goal 1: Financially & Prayerfully Supporting Middle East Missions

Specific

By April 9, 2027, I will have contributed $450 to a friend doing long-term missions in the Middle East, and will pray for him once per week.

Measurable

I will put time on my calendar to pray for him every Wednesday and set up a recurring monthly payment through Cru.

Achievable

By August 15, I need to set up monthly payments.

Relevant

I believe people are either senders or goers to the nations, and right now I can send. I want to be part of the gospel moving in the Middle East through my friend.

Time-bound

By April 9, 2027, I will have donated $450 to my friend, with my mentor checking in monthly on how my prayer for him is going.

Goal 2: Building a Relationship with Indian Bible Church

Specific

By March 7, 2027, I will have met with Venu from Indian Bible Church two times and visited his church once, to better understand how to relate the gospel to Indians.

Measurable

I will schedule two meetings with Venu and one visit to his church, and ask my mentor to hold me accountable.

Achievable

By November 1, schedule my first meeting with Venu. By January 6, schedule my second meeting. By February 7, schedule my church visit.

Relevant

We have a significant Indian/Hindu population in our neighborhoods and communities that we want to reach with the gospel. Meeting with Venu will give me a better context for how to interact with a different culture.

Time-bound

By November 14, met with Venu once. By January 22, met with him a second time. By March 7, visited Indian Bible Church.
