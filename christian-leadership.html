SMART Goals

Goal 1: Understanding Our Church's Multiplication DNA

Specific

To better understand how to have multiplication and sending be a part of our church's DNA, I will read Gaining by Losing by J.D. Greear by March 1, 2027, and review how this DNA does or does not exist in our ministries with my mentor.

Measurable

I will schedule time on my calendar and buy the book, Gaining by Losing by J.D. Greear.

Achievable

I will read for 30 minutes twice per week, scheduling that time as a recurring block on my calendar, starting December 28.

Relevant

Our church wants to be a sending and multiplying church, so reading and reflecting on this book will help me see how that is and isn't happening currently.

Time-bound

I will finish reading Gaining by Losing by March 1, and complete a deep dive into our church and its ministries with my mentor by March 22.

Goal 2: Developing a Philosophy of Small Groups

Specific

To better understand the Small Groups ministry I'll inherit in my new role, I will develop a philosophy of small groups by March 1, 2027.

Measurable

I will schedule meetings with Small Group Directors from different churches, using Dr. Hillman's notes on Groups as a resource.

Achievable

I will meet (in person or by video call) with three different Small Group Directors, and read Dr. Hillman's class notes on Groups for 30 minutes on Fridays for one month.

Relevant

Small Groups are one of the engines of multiplication at our church. Knowing how best to approach groups in our context will help our people grow and see others reached.

Time-bound

By November 1, met with one church leader. By December 1, read Dr. Hillman's notes. By February 1, met with two church leaders. By March 1, met with three church leaders. By April 1, written a philosophy of Small Groups.
